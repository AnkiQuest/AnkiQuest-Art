# Archive

Pictures that are no longer current masters, kept byte for byte because
companion art is never deleted. Nothing here is exported to a client.

- `aki/cards/`, `ankilope/cards/`: hand-made 512 px share-card copies
  AnkiQuest-Server embedded before its build exported card art from the
  masters (formerly `assets/cards/` there).
- `cow/`: Bluma's first master names and two superseded add-on exports;
  see its README.
- `anki-tan/`: earlier generated Anki-tan poses and superseded add-on
  exports; see its README. CC BY-SA 4.0 under its LICENSE.
- `anki-tan-pack/`: the rest of Shigeyuki's Anki-tan asset pack; see its
  README. CC BY-SA 4.0 under its LICENSE.

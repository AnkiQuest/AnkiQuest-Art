# Warm blankets and Aki’s second frozen day

The warm blanket is the `freeze` picture for Ankilope, Bluma and full-size
Anki-tan. They use Aki’s pale-blue snowflake blanket and a calm expression.
Aki’s new `frozen_2` pose adds frost and icicles while he is still standing.
The third-day ice block stays in `aki/frozen_3.png`. The brief for each
companion describes the remaining freeze, fire and away pictures.
[Aki](https://github.com/AnkiQuest/AnkiQuest-Art/issues/23) ·
[Ankilope](https://github.com/AnkiQuest/AnkiQuest-Art/issues/24) ·
[Bluma](https://github.com/AnkiQuest/AnkiQuest-Art/issues/25) ·
[Anki-tan](https://github.com/AnkiQuest/AnkiQuest-Art/issues/26)

Aki’s former `frozen.png` moves to `freeze.png`. His former `frozen_2.png`
moves to `frozen.png` and serves both the first frozen day and `freeze_used`.
Both existing files keep their exact bytes.

The [prompts](prompts.md) record the references used with OpenAI’s built-in
image generation. The tool does not report its model identifier. The four
new masters are on transparent 1254 × 1254 canvases with the drawings fitted
to the centered 1050 × 1050 area. The [measurements](measurements.json)
record the bounding boxes and SHA-256 hashes. The poses were compared with
their neighbors at 64 px on light and dark backgrounds.

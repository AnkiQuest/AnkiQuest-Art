# Deck conquered and freeze used

The six poses fill two moment slots for Ankilope, Bluma and Anki-tan
in [AnkiQuest-Art #16](https://github.com/AnkiQuest/AnkiQuest-Art/issues/16).
Deck conquered uses the gold pennant and flashcards from Aki's version
of the moment. Freeze used shows relief and thawing ice.

The [generation record](generation.json) lists the 18 prompts, their
reference art and the six selected pictures. OpenAI's built-in image
generation did not expose its model identifier. The 1254 × 1254
transparent RGBA masters were checked at 72 px on light and dark
backgrounds. The [measurements](measurements.json) record pixel bounds,
hashes and the [alpha cleanup](../alpha-cleanup/README.md).
